- **S3 — OpenCode full `gemini-4-*`, `xhigh`, `3.5-flash-lite`, & `gpt-oss-120b-medium` support (`src/`)**:
  - Support `xhigh` in `ThinkingTier` and `ModelThinkingLevel`.
  - Resolve bare and `antigravity-`-prefixed `gemini-4-argon`, `gemini-4-carbon`, `gemini-4-neon`, `gemini-4-hydrogen` (and explicit `-low`, `-medium`, `-high`, `-xhigh`, `-tiered-high`, `-tiered-xhigh`, `-sonar` wire IDs) to verified Cloud Code wire IDs (`gemini-4-argon-high` / `gemini-4-carbon-high` / `gemini-4-neon-high` / `gemini-4-hydrogen-high` defaults when bare; exact tier wire ID when tier or variant is specified), without mangling `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` or `gpt-oss-120b-medium`.
  - Extend Gemini 3+ checks across `gemini.ts`, `request.ts`, `request-helpers.ts`, `quota.ts` (`classifyQuotaGroup`), `config/models.ts` (`OPENCODE_MODEL_DEFINITIONS` with `262144` max output for Carbon/Neon/Hydrogen and `65536` for Argon), and exclude `gemini-4-*` from public Gemini API-key fallback in `api-key.ts`.
- **S4 — Pi Hub headers, `gemini-4-*` catalog/envelope, `TranscriptContext` (`v1.4.0+`) compatibility, & Pi harness prompt sanitization (`packages/pi/`)**:
  - Replace hardcoded `antigravity/cli/1.1.23` in `packages/pi/src/stream.ts` and `packages/pi/src/project.ts` with `formatAntigravityHubUserAgent()` from `@benjamolina/antigravity-guard-core`.
